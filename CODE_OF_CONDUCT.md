# Code of Conduct

ThreadPort welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and accept corrections gracefully.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and make room for others to participate.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For project discussions, flag the relevant issue or pull request and contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/AristideDongo). Do not post private information in a public issue. For abuse that requires a confidential report, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant comment or account. The maintainer may remove content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation as appropriate. GitHub handles reports submitted through its reporting tools under its own policies.
